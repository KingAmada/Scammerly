5. Empty Reports Do Not Mean Safe

NO REPORT FOUND does not mean NO RISK.

If a search or Full Report returns no complaints, no verified reports, no evidence-backed reports or otherwise appears empty, that does not mean the person, seller, account holder, business or transaction is legitimate.

It may simply mean Scamenot has not yet received relevant information concerning that identity.

  • A person who has never been reported can still commit fraud.
  • A newly created phone number or bank account can still be used for fraud.
  • A scammer may change phone numbers, accounts, names, websites or social-media pages.
  • Scamenot cannot know about every transaction or scam that has occurred.

Users must continue to exercise independent caution even where Scamenot has no adverse information.

6. A Populated Report Is Not Proof of Guilt

The existence of reports, evidence, repeated searches or other signals does not automatically mean the subject is a scammer.

Search activity is not proof of fraud. A statement such as “15 checks on Scamenot” means the identifier has been checked that number of times within the platform's records. It does not necessarily mean 15 different people checked it and does not mean 15 complaints exist.
